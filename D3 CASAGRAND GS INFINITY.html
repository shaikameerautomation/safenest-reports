Questions worth asking before you book

These are not warnings — they are things we found during due diligence that deserve a direct conversation with the developer before you sign anything.

Historical Unregistered Documents in Title Chain
During our review, we identified three historical property instruments that were not registered at the time they were executed: a 1968 family partition deed, a 1993 Family Arrangement, and a 1993 Agreement. The developer's advocate has reviewed these and opines that the current title is valid; our concern is that unregistered instruments in the primary title chain can sometimes surface as complications during future resale or home loan disbursement. There is also an unresolved ULC extent discrepancy across four different measurement sources (the HMDA, RERA, Sale Deeds, and ULC records show different land areas). These items alone do not make the project unbookable — but they do make an independent legal review essential before you sign the Agreement of Sale.
What to do: Before signing the Agreement of Sale, engage your own property advocate (not the developer's) to review the title chain and specifically examine the 1968 and 1993 instruments, and to give you an independent opinion on the ULC extent discrepancy.
Joint Venture DAGPA Registration Not Confirmed
The project is a joint venture between Casagrand Hyderwise Private Limited (developer) and G.S.G. Builders & Infrastructure Limited (land owner) under a Development Agreement cum General Power of Attorney (DAGPA). The TSRERA listing cites this DAGPA, but the registration document number is not available in the documents we reviewed. A DAGPA that is not registered at the Sub-Registrar's office carries a higher JV risk — in Hyderabad, registered DAPGAs are a key protection for buyers because they establish the developer's authority to sell units on the land legally. This is worth confirming before you book.
What to do: Ask the sales team for the DAGPA document number and the Sub-Registrar's office where it was registered. Cross-verify by searching the document number on the IGRS Telangana portal before proceeding.
Foundation Stage — 36-Month Horizon
All RERA-reported work-done percentages for CASAGRAND GS INFINITY are currently at 0%, meaning the building has not yet risen above ground. The builder timeline states possession in 36 months from your date of agreement, and the RERA expiry is April 2030 — giving a reasonable buffer, but no construction buffer if delays occur. Buying at Foundation Stage means your money tracks construction over three years rather than going into a near-complete building; this is the normal CLP risk, but it is material. The mitigating factor is the confirmed 3-account ICICI Bank escrow, which ensures your money is ring-fenced for this project's construction and cannot be freely diverted.
What to do: Before each CLP payment tranche, visit the site or ask your SafeNest advisor to verify that physical construction has progressed to the milestone stated in your payment schedule. Check TSRERA quarterly progress reports are being filed regularly.
Mortgage Area on Floors 18–26 and NOC Updates for 36-Floor Height
TSRERA flags a mortgage area covering floors 18–26 (11,139 sqm), but no deed number is provided — the nature and terms of this mortgage are not confirmed in our reviewed documents. What happens if they delay possession?
Under Telangana RERA, if the developer misses the possession date in your Agreement of Sale, you are entitled to compensation at your home loan interest rate for every month of delay — this is a legal right you do not need to negotiate for separately. You can also choose to exit the project and receive a full refund with interest. The RERA expiry date for this project is April 2030, which gives a reasonable buffer beyond the 36-month builder timeline; however, at Foundation Stage there is no construction buffer yet accumulated. A delay costs the builder money, not just you time.
Is the price fair for what you get?
At ₹8,172 per sft (CLP), the pricing is competitive for what is being offered — Mivan construction, 10.66 ft ceilings, 32-ft triple-height lobby, 3.16 parking slots per unit, and 101 sqft of clubhouse per home is a specification bundle that typically commands ₹10,000–12,000 per sft in comparable West Hyderabad micro-markets. Attapur is an established inner-city address but does not carry the same ORR-corridor premium, which is why this price point is achievable. Whether that trade-off — lower price, inner-city address, slightly longer commute to ORR — works for you depends on where you work and how you value the neighbourhood.

Where we found differences between the brochure and official records

In preparing this report, we cross-checked the developer's brochure and price sheet against TSRERA filings, HMDA approval records, Encumbrance Certificates, and independent legal opinions. Where we found differences on facts that may matter to your decision, we list them here so you can ask the sales team directly.

Floor Count
The brochure states "3B + G + 35 Floors." HMDA and RERA both record "3 Basements + 36 Slabs of Super Structure." We used 36 floors per the HMDA/RERA approved structural count — this is the regulatory figure that governs the build.
What to ask the sales team: Confirm the approved floor count is 36 super-structure slabs as per HMDA building permission 005919/GHMC/2844/CHR1/2024-BP.
Land Area — Material Discrepancy
Four different source documents show four different land areas: ULC proceedings = 15,264 sqm; Sale Deeds = 16,749 sqm; HMDA net area = 17,781 sqm; RERA/HMDA total = 19,412 sqm (4.80 acres). We used the RERA/HMDA figure as primary, but this four-source discrepancy is material and should be resolved by your advocate during the title review.
What to ask the sales team: Which land area figure is reflected in the final sale deed, and can they provide the ULC order clarifying the extent?
Minimum Unit Size
The launch flyer lists 3BHK starting at 2,386 sft; the Product Mix document lists 2,375 sft as the minimum. We used 2,375 sft (the lower, more conservative figure from the Product Mix). Confirm the exact carpet area and super built-up area for your specific unit before signing.
What to ask the sales team: Request the RERA-filed carpet area for your specific unit type — this is the legally binding measurement.
Ceiling Height Clarification
The 10.66 ft figure in this report is the floor-to-floor height noted in project documents. The true clear ceiling height (after slab deduction) may be approximately 10.0–10.2 ft — still significantly above the 9 ft market standard, but worth confirming from the structural section drawing.
What to ask the sales team: Request the clear ceiling height (floor-to-ceiling, not floor-to-floor) from the structural drawings.
Open Space Ratio Not Confirmed
The brochure claims 75% open area as a marketing figure. This was not confirmed from the HMDA layout plan in the documents we reviewed, and we left this field blank in our data rather than reproduce an unverified marketing claim.
What to ask the sales team: Request the HMDA-approved layout plan showing the open space calculation.
